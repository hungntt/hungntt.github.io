Short Bio

C:\HUNG\README.TXT

I am currently a PhD candidate at Analytics Everywhere Lab, University of New Brunswick (UNB), Canada and Research Assistant at National Research Council Canada (NRC-CNRC), Canada, under the supervision of Prof. Hung Cao and Rene Richard in January 2024.

I received my Master degree of Artificial Intelligence at Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU), Germany in 2023 and my Bachelor degree of Computer Science at Frankfurt University of Applied Sciences (FRA-UAS), Germany and Vietnamese-German University (VGU), Vietnam in 2020.

My research interest involves Self-Evolving Multi-agent Systems, Contestable/Explainable Artificial Intelligence (C/XAI), Computer Vision, Edge Computing, Biomedical Signal/Image Analysis, Quantum Machine Learning, and Reinforcement Learning.
